Mountain Bike Hire in Wilderness Forest

Adult and junior bikes are available for 4 hours or a full day so visitors can explore the trails of the Scottish Borders.

Hire rates

Prices for 4-hour and full-day hire for adult and junior bikes.

Bike type 4 hours Full day
Adult hardtail MTB £15 £25
Adult road bike £12 £20
Junior hardtail MTB £12 £20

Hire rules

  • ID required: passport or driving licence, plus a credit card deposit.
  • Over-18s must sign for and supervise riders under 16.
  • Riders aged 16-17 can ride unsupervised but cannot supervise under-16s.
  • All bikes must be returned by 5 pm or a second day may be charged.

Good to know

Need equipment before you ride? You can also visit our bike shop for accessories or contact our team for trail advice and availability.
