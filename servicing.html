Service menu

Choose the service that best matches your bike. If you are unsure, our staff can help you pick the right option.

Gear service £25

A full strip down and rebuild of the drivetrain. Our mechanics clean, check and lubricate all parts and recommend any replacements required.

  • Full bike inspection and written report
  • Gear cables checked for stretch and wear
  • Chain checked for stretch and wear
  • Chain and derailleurs removed and cleaned
  • Chain or cables replaced if required (parts not included)
  • All parts refitted and lubricated
  • Gears fully re-indexed

Brake service £20

Designed to keep your brakes working reliably. Suitable for V-brakes, cable disc brakes and hydraulic systems.

  • Full bike inspection and written report
  • Cables or hoses checked for wear and leaks
  • Pad condition checked and pads replaced if required
  • Braking surfaces cleaned and rotors straightened where needed
  • Brake system bled or topped up when required
  • Full caliper alignment
  • Full brake setup and adjustment

Wheel service £25

A wheel service focused on smooth running and hub performance, with wheel alignment and spoke tension checks.

  • Full bike inspection and written report
  • Wheels cleaned
  • Hubs stripped, bearings checked, cleaned and greased
  • Wheel alignment and spoke tension adjusted
  • Broken spokes replaced where possible (up to 3 spokes free)
  • Tyres checked for cuts, damage and wear
  • Full wheel rebuild available for £10 per wheel
